# Companion - Voice: Shelley

Voice pack for Companion (requires Companion 0.72+): Shelley's full voice (higher, upbeat and playful). Adds her 1,052 zone comments and zone histories; her everyday lines are already built into Companion. Note: 685 of these clips are temporary stand-ins (Kahani's recordings raised in pitch) until they are re-recorded in Shelley's own voice.

**Version 0.72** · Interface 16001 · WoW: Forever · by Darth

## Install with WowUp

1. In WowUp, pick your WoW: Forever installation.
2. Go to **Get Addons** → **Install from URL**.
3. Paste `https://github.com/nomuno/Companion_Voice_Shelley` and install. WowUp checks this repo's releases for updates.

## Manual install

Download `Companion_Voice_Shelley-0.72-forever.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/nomuno/Companion_Voice_Shelley/releases/latest) and unzip it into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns`.

## What's new in 0.72

- First release. Shelley's zone comments (790) and zone and landmark histories (262), moved out of the main Companion download.
- Unzip into Interface/AddOns next to Companion and restart WoW fully. Settings > AddOns > Companion > Audio shows it as Installed - full pack.
- 685 of the clips are temporary stand-ins (Kahani's voice raised in pitch, from 0.71). They will be re-recorded in Shelley's own voice in a later version.
